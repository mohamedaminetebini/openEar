# Manufacturer assets

`redragon.svg` is the unmodified Redragon logo downloaded on 2026-10-10 from the [official Redragon store](https://redragonshop.com/).

Source: https://redragonshop.com/cdn/shop/files/logo_688c7da5-4233-4e36-a63d-429777c57159.svg?v=1729738782&width=600

Redragon's logo and trademarks belong to their respective owners and are excluded from Openear's MIT license. Openear is an independent application, not an official manufacturer utility. Assets are bundled locally; the app makes no runtime requests to manufacturer sites.

The theme uses the source logo's red `#E60012` and white. A lighter red is used for text and focus indicators to maintain contrast on dark surfaces.
